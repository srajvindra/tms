❯ what is PERSON_STOP still not clear                                           

● PERSON_STOP is a stop-word list used to avoid misclassifying Wikipedia
  articles as people.

  Here's the problem it solves. The classifier has a heuristic in
  isWikipediaPersonTitle() (UrlClassifier.php:240) that says: a Wikipedia title
  of 2–4 capitalized words with no digits is probably a person's name — like
  "Rahul Sharma" or "Sam Altman". That pattern would classify these as
  individuals.

  But lots of non-person Wikipedia titles also look exactly like that: "Taj
  Mahal", "Ganga River", "Mysore Palace", "Apache Kafka", "Delhi University" —
  all 2–4 capitalized words. Without a filter, they'd all be tagged as people.

  So PERSON_STOP is a whitespace-separated list of words that signal "this title
  is NOT a person":

  - places/geography: river, mountain, lake, city, state, district
  - institutions: university, college, school, institute
  - landmarks: temple, fort, palace, museum, bridge, airport
  - events/history: war, battle, dynasty, empire, election, treaty
  - media: film, movie, series, album, song
  - tech-ish traps: apache, query, power (so "Apache Kafka" or "Power Query"
  isn't a person)
  - plus plain grammar words: the, of, and, in, for…

  How it's used: in the constructor it's converted to a lookup set
  ($this->personStopWords). Then isWikipediaPersonTitle() lowercases each word
  of the title and checks it against the set — if any word is in the list, the
  title is rejected as a person (UrlClassifier.php:253). It's also used at line
  228 for single-word titles: "Infosys" (capitalized, not a stop word) → private
  or corporate, but "Temple" (a stop word) → falls through to Other.

  That's why the test at tests/Unit/UrlClassifierTest.php:64 asserts "Taj Mahal
  Palace - Wikipedia" is not classified as individuals — "palace" is in the stop
  list.

  It stayed hardcoded during the DB refactor because it's an internal tuning
  detail of the heuristic, not category data — but it could be moved to the
  database too (e.g. as a Keyword row) if you want it editable.
